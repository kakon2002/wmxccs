Sent as client_reference. Re-using one is refused with 409: a frozen prediction is never overwritten.
The service takes composition, adduct and charge and nothing else. A prediction is a function of those, which is why it can be frozen the moment it is made.

The prototype offered these on this page. None of them changes which structures are enumerated or how they are banded, so the service does not accept them here and this dashboard does not pretend to send them. Drift gas, instrument and calibrant are real on the Experimental Validation page, where they describe a measurement.
